## Facts and sources (2+ each)
- Lights/wiring involved in >2 in 5 home Christmas tree fires: NFPA press release Dec 2024 (prweb) ("electrical distribution or lighting equipment involved in more than two in five"); NC OSFM ("Electrical problems are the source of nearly half").
- Watered tree resists a flame, dry tree ignites readily: NIST news 2010 ("if a well-watered Fraser fir briefly ignites, the flame soon dies"); NIST Fire Safety for the Holidays ("A single match could not ignite the tree").
- Dry trees fully ablaze in under 30 s in lab tests: NIST ("A dry Scotch Pine Tree takes less than nine seconds to become fully involved in flames"); WPI 2016 ("Within 25 seconds, the branches were fully engulfed").
- Flashover in about a minute: WPI ("flashover occurred within 63 seconds"; definition: simultaneous ignition of exposed combustibles); NIST (dry trees can produce heat release "capable of causing flashover in residential scale rooms").
- Room can top 1,000 °F: UL FSRI Close Before You Doze (open-door rooms "1000+ degrees"); FSRI 2022 release ("In just three minutes or less, a room could be more than 1,000 degrees Fahrenheit"); NAR quotes the same. Comparison: home ovens top out around 500–550 °F → "about twice as hot as an oven".
- 17 minutes to escape ~30 years ago vs ~3 today (UL research): News4Jax 2016 ("17 minutes… just about three minutes"); TODAY/AARP 2016 ("down to three or four minutes"); NAR 2022 ("three to four minutes… about 17 minutes"). Script hedges: "it can be closer to three".
- Smoke kills more than flames (smoke inhalation the leading cause of home-fire deaths): FSRI 2022 release (Kerber: "Smoke inhalation is the leading cause of death in home fires"); USFA smoke-alarm guidance.
- Closed door: closed rooms <100 °F vs 1,000+ °F open (UL FSRI Close Before You Doze; FSRI 2022 release: "a room with a closed door may only reach 100 degrees").
- More than a third (36%) of home Christmas tree fires occur in January (NFPA 2018–2022): Fire & Safety Journal Americas, Jan 2025; Security Sales & Integration, Jan 2025.
- Tips (water daily, 3 ft from heat, lights off at night/when leaving): NC OSFM; WPI; Penn State Extension (replenish water daily).
- Fiction: the house, the cast, the dialogue and Leo's rescue.

Cut as unverifiable / inconsistent: per-year death counts (NFPA figures vary 1–10/yr by period), "1 in every 9 tree fires is fatal" (2002–05 data only), "burns faster than newspaper" (one source).
